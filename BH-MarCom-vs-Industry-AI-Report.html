Executive Summary
BH MarCom is not just ahead of the industry average. The department outperforms the most advanced segment in the entire SmarterX dataset: CEOs and Founders.
The 2026 State of AI for Business Report surveyed 2,109 professionals across roles, industries, and company sizes. It found that CEOs and Founders are the most advanced personal AI adopters, the most confident evaluators, and the most positive about AI's trajectory. They are the top of the distribution. BH MarCom, as an entire department, beats that cohort on three of four core metrics:

The SmarterX report identifies four governance foundations required to scale AI responsibly: an AI council, an AI roadmap, generative AI policies, and an AI ethics policy. Only 13% of organizations have all four. BH MarCom has all four. But 13% still understates the department's position, because most of that 13% doesn't have the adoption numbers to match. Among organizations with all four governance foundations, 50% report accelerating momentum. Among those, few would also show 90% daily use, 74% in Integration/Transformation, and 69% positive sentiment simultaneously.
Our assessment: BH MarCom's combined profile - adoption maturity, sentiment, confidence, daily use, tool ecosystem, governance foundations, and 14-month trajectory - places this department in the top 5% of the 2,109 professionals and organizations represented in this report. The department doesn't just compare favorably to the average. It outperforms the ceiling.

Section 10

Key Takeaways

Where BH MarCom Leads

The department is 17-21 percentage points ahead of the industry average on adoption, confidence, and sentiment. It outperforms even the CEO/Founder cohort - the most advanced segment in the entire report. Daily use at 90%, 74% in Integration/Transformation, all four governance foundations in place. Combined profile places BH MarCom in the top 5% of the 2,109 professionals surveyed.

Where to Watch

The department's remaining challenges mirror what the SmarterX report identifies as the barriers that mature organizations face: lack of time (the #1 barrier at the Scaling stage, cited by 42%), tool access friction, and uneven adoption across functions. Some teams are in Transformation while others are still in Experimentation. The 25% who describe momentum as "inconsistent" need targeted support - the AI Labs office hours are designed for exactly this.

The Opportunity

BH MarCom is doing what 58% of the industry WANTS to learn how to do. The department has a credible case to lead AI adoption not just within Marketing, but as a model for the broader organization. The governance is built, the adoption is real, and the AI Labs provide the ongoing support structure. The next step is scaling what works here across the system.
